# Reviews and feedback

The review section uses a familiar avatar/star/card layout, with six clearly labelled fictional sample reviews and varied 3-, 4- and 5-star ratings. They are not presented as real customers, verified testimonials or Google reviews. Sample names are Sri Lankan Sinhala-style names written in English letters.

The form accepts real visitor names, any 1–5 star rating and feedback. It saves a private browser draft and offers an email link to `polwaththahardware2024@gmail.com`. The visitor must send that email from their own email app. Nothing is submitted automatically, and the site does not claim a review has been published or sent to Google.

There is no shared review database or Google review integration. To publish website reviews across visitors, connect a moderated backend and obtain the reviewer’s consent to show their name. Genuine Google reviews must be submitted to the actual Google Business listing, not manufactured by this website.

Browser storage can be unavailable in private mode or restricted local previews. The form catches those errors and still provides the email option. Stored data is validated and rendered as text, not HTML.

The supplied map URL currently opens a personal live-location share rather than a Google Business listing. Do not expose the live-tracking URL or claim it is the shop’s address until the owner confirms the exact static pin.

The contact section instead embeds the official public **Periya Ulukkulama** locality map, obtained from Google Maps → Share → Embed a map. It is explicitly labelled an area map, not an exact shop pin. It loads lazily and has a direct Maps link as fallback. Replace it with the confirmed shop embed after receiving a permanent pin or business listing.
